**Purpose:** Give each entity 2 to 5 short tags. Entities that share a tag become linked, so a good
tag is a term other entities could share.

**Steps for each entity:**
1. Role tag (always): what the text says the entity does or is, as a general term, e.g.
   "Project Lead", "Event Venue", "Funding Source", "Required Training".
2. Kind tag (one or two): what kind of thing it is, more specific than its type, e.g.
   "Public Library", "Investment Bank", "Marathon Runner".

**Never:**
- the type itself ("Person", "City", "Organization")
- a name inside a tag ("Maria's Project")
- numbers or dates ("2024", "Age 41")
- sentences ("Leads the project in Berlin")

**Tag format:** 1 to 4 words, Capitalized Like A Heading.

**Example:** "Maria Lopez leads Project Alpha." gives Maria Lopez: "Project Lead", "Program Leadership";
Project Alpha: "Led Project", "Initiative".
